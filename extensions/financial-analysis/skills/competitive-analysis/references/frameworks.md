# Frameworks Reference

## 2x2 Matrix: Common Axis Pairs by Industry

_Technology/SaaS:_ Product breadth × Customer segment, Integration depth × Geographic reach

_Consumer/Retail:_ Price point × Product range, Online × Offline presence

_Financial Services:_ Product complexity × Customer sophistication, Scale × Specialization

_Healthcare:_ Care setting × Payer mix, Technology enablement × Service breadth

_Industrial:_ Customization × Scale, Geographic scope × Vertical focus
